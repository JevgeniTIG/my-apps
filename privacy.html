Privacy Policy

Catch It All! · Last updated 25 August 2026

Catch It All! is developed by Jevgeni Agnevstsikov. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it does not collect any.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing. The app collects no personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. Nothing you do in the game is transmitted to us, and we operate no servers that could receive it.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

The app saves your best score for each location and which locations you have unlocked. This is stored only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

PURCHASES

Locations beyond the free one are one-time purchases processed entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase succeeded, so that the location can be unlocked. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

CHILDREN

The game is suitable for children. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either. It contains no advertising and no social features. The only links that lead out of the app are App Store pages for our other games, on the More Games screen.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new date.
