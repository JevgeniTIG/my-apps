Catch It All!

A one-tap catching game for families

GET IT ON THE APP STORE

HOW IT WORKS

The game is built out of locations — separate worlds, each with its own setting, its own playable characters, its own things to catch and its own cartoon at the end.

One location is free and always will be. You can play it start to finish without paying anything. Every further location is a separate one-time purchase, yours permanently once bought. There is no subscription, no currency and no timer.

LOCATIONS

FREE
Egg Farm — catch eggs rolling off the shelves. Four characters to choose from.
PURCHASE
Pirate Ship — catch gold coins into a treasure chest. Three characters, its own reward cartoon.

More locations are in the works. New ones appear in Menu → Locations as they are released.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Catch the falling items. They roll off the shelves on both sides and drop. Use the four buttons to move left or right, up or down.
  2. You start with 3 lives. Every item that hits the ground costs one.
  3. Earn lives back. After 20 catches, ten in a row without a miss returns a life, up to three.
  4. Catch 100 to finish a location and unlock its cartoon.

QUESTIONS

I paid for a location but it is locked.

Open Menu → Locations and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so this also works on a new device.

Do I have to pay to play?

No. One location is free and complete — characters, cartoon and all. Paying only adds further locations.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on the device. A connection is only needed the moment you buy or restore a location.

Are there ads?

None. The only thing that costs money is unlocking a location.

Is it safe to hand to a child?

There are no ads, no accounts and no data collection. The only links that leave the game are the App Store pages on the More Games screen.

The purchase failed or was charged twice.

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Use reportaproblem.apple.com, and write to us as well so we can help.
