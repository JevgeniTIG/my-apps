Yet Another Sea Battle

Place your fleet, find theirs first

GET IT ON THE APP STORE

ABOUT

Classic naval combat: place your ships, then hunt the opponent's fleet before they sink yours.

Play against the computer at several difficulty levels, hand one device back and forth in Pass & Play, or connect two devices on the same Wi-Fi network.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Place your fleet. Drag ships onto the grid and rotate them until you are happy.
  2. Take turns firing. Pick a cell on the opponent's grid. Hits, misses and sunk ships are marked for you.
  3. Sink everything to win.

QUESTIONS

I made a purchase but it is not there.

Look for Restore Purchases in the app. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so this also works on a new device.

Does the game need an internet connection?

Single player and Pass & Play work with no connection at all. Two-device play sends moves directly between the two devices on your local Wi-Fi network — there is no server in between and nothing is stored online.

Are there ads?

No. None of our games show ads.

The purchase failed or was charged twice.

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Use reportaproblem.apple.com, and write to us as well so we can help.
