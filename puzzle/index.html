Yet Another Puzzle

Turn any photo into a jigsaw

GET IT ON THE APP STORE

ABOUT

Pick a picture from your camera roll, snap a new one, or import any image, and it becomes a real jigsaw in seconds.

Choose the piece count and a shattering effect — glass, fire, electric, shockwave or ice — then solve it yourself or send the puzzle to someone else.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Pick a photo. From your camera roll, the camera, or any image you import.
  2. Choose piece count and effect. More pieces, more evenings.
  3. Solve it or hand it to someone else to solve.

QUESTIONS

I made a purchase but it is not there.

Look for Restore Purchases in the app. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so this also works on a new device.

Does the game need an internet connection?

Your photos stay on your device — they are never uploaded to us. A connection is only needed for purchases.

Are there ads?

No. None of our games show ads.

The purchase failed or was charged twice.

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Use reportaproblem.apple.com, and write to us as well so we can help.
