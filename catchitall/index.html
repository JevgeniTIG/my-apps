Catch It All!

Catch what falls before it lands

GET IT ON THE APP STORE

ABOUT

The game is built out of locations — separate worlds, each with its own setting, playable characters, things to catch and a cartoon at the end.

One location is free and always will be. You can play it from beginning to end without paying. Every further location is a separate one-time purchase, yours permanently once bought. No subscription, no currency, no timer.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Catch the falling items. They roll off the shelves on both sides and drop. Four buttons move you left or right, up or down.
  2. You start with 3 lives. Every item that hits the ground costs one.
  3. Earn lives back. After 20 catches, ten in a row without a miss returns a life.
  4. Catch 100 to finish a location and unlock its cartoon.

QUESTIONS

I made a purchase but it is not there.

Look for Restore Purchases in the app. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so this also works on a new device.

Does the game need an internet connection?

The game runs entirely on your device. A connection is only needed the moment you buy or restore a location.

Are there ads?

No. None of our games show ads.

The purchase failed or was charged twice.

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Use reportaproblem.apple.com, and write to us as well so we can help.
