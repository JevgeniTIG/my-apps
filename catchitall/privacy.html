Privacy Policy

Catch It All! · Last updated 25 August 2026

Catch It All! is developed by Jevgeni Agnevstsikov. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it does not collect any.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing. The app collects no personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. We operate no servers that could receive your data.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

The app saves your best score for each location, and which locations you have unlocked. This is stored only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

CONNECTIVITY

The game runs entirely on your device. A connection is only needed the moment you buy or restore a location.

PURCHASES

Locations beyond the free one are one-time purchases. Payments are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your payment details — Apple tells the app only whether a purchase succeeded. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

CHILDREN

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either. There is no advertising and there are no social features.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new date.
