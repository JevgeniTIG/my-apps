Smash My Doodle

Draw your enemy, then smash it

GET IT ON THE APP STORE

ABOUT

Stress relief, roughly. Draw whoever is bothering you, then destroy the drawing with a barrage of food projectiles before the timer runs out.

Nine levels and a final boss. No connection needed.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Draw your target. Anything you like — it becomes the thing you smash.
  2. Smash it. Fling projectiles at it before time runs out.
  3. Clear nine levels and the boss at the end.

QUESTIONS

I made a purchase but it is not there.

Look for Restore Purchases in the app. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so this also works on a new device.

Does the game need an internet connection?

The game runs on your device. A connection is only needed for purchases.

Are there ads?

No. None of our apps show ads.

The purchase failed or was charged twice.

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Use reportaproblem.apple.com, and write to us as well so we can help.
